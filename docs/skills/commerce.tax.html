commerce.tax

价税分离

Round the supplied amount to cents and calculate tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive amounts from the caller's rate; does not determine applicable tax rules.

Version 1.0.0确定性数据处理Backend required

使用场景 / Purpose

Commerce: tax. 本技能对提供的参数执行计算，输入输出契约如下。文档例子来自已实现函数的固定验收样例；网页本身不执行代码。
